## Projects

### [MTG Pack Opener](https://github.com/desidj27/MTG-Web-Port)

Open virtual Magic: The Gathering packs, flip through the reveal, and save what you pull. Card data comes from Scryfall, with pack art from the MTG Wiki. Live at [mtg.daviddesing.website](https://mtg.daviddesing.website/).

### [What Is Above Me](https://github.com/desidj27/WhatIsAboveMe)

Point it at your location and it shows the satellites currently overhead. Search by name or NORAD ID, draw ground tracks, find upcoming visual passes, and explore them on a 3D globe. Live at [what-is-above-me.daviddesing.website](https://what-is-above-me.daviddesing.website/).

### [StatX](https://github.com/desidj27/StatX)

Discord server analytics. Tracks messages, voice time, and game activity, then puts it in a dashboard admins can actually use. Each server gets its own rankings, feature toggles, and settings. Live at [statx.website](https://statx.website/).

### [Ranked Minesweeper](https://github.com/desidj27/Minesweeper)

1v1 minesweeper on a shared board. Clear it first, or survive after your opponent hits a mine. Wins and losses move your ELO. Live at [minesweeper.daviddesing.website](https://minesweeper.daviddesing.website/).

### [BIS Loot](https://github.com/desidj27/BIS-Loot)

Marketplace tracker for Darker and Darker. Live listings, price history, underpriced-deal alerts, and a crafting calculator priced against current market data. Live at [bisloot.website](https://www.bisloot.website/).
