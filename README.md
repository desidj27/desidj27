# David Desing

Personal site for [David Desing](https://daviddesing.website) — about, projects, and links. Computer science student at the University of Wisconsin–Green Bay, focused on software engineering. Most of the recent work is TypeScript and Next.js: dashboards, market tools, and small products that sit on top of real data.

[Live site](https://daviddesing.website) · [GitHub](https://github.com/desidj27) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/david-desing-606748233)

## Run locally

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000).

## Repos

### [website](https://github.com/desidj27/website)

This repo. A Next.js site that collects the projects below, with a short about section and links out to GitHub and LinkedIn. Live at [daviddesing.website](https://daviddesing.website).

### [MTG-Web-Port](https://github.com/desidj27/MTG-Web-Port)

Open virtual Magic: The Gathering packs, flip through the reveal, and save what you pull. Card data comes from Scryfall, with pack art from the MTG Wiki. Live at [mtg.daviddesing.website](https://mtg.daviddesing.website/).

### [WhatIsAboveMe](https://github.com/desidj27/WhatIsAboveMe)

Point it at your location and it shows the satellites currently overhead. Search by name or NORAD ID, draw ground tracks, find upcoming visual passes, and explore them on a 3D globe using N2YO and CelesTrak. Live at [what-is-above-me.daviddesing.website](https://what-is-above-me.daviddesing.website/).

### [StatX](https://github.com/desidj27/StatX)

Discord server analytics. Tracks messages, voice time, and game activity, then puts it in a dashboard admins can actually use. Each server gets its own rankings, feature toggles, and settings. Live at [statx.website](https://statx.website/).

### [Minesweeper](https://github.com/desidj27/Minesweeper)

Ranked 1v1 minesweeper. Both players race on the same mine layout. Clear the board first, or survive after your opponent hits a mine. Wins and losses move your ELO. Live at [minesweeper.daviddesing.website](https://minesweeper.daviddesing.website/).

### [BIS-Loot](https://github.com/desidj27/BIS-Loot)

Marketplace tracker for Darker and Darker. Live listings, historical price charts, underpriced-deal alerts, and a crafting calculator that prices recipes against current market data from DarkerDB. Live at [bisloot.website](https://www.bisloot.website/).

### [RobloxGame](https://github.com/desidj27/RobloxGame)

Mine & Reel, a Roblox mining and fishing simulator written in Luau with Rojo. Mine ore and fish for coins, hatch pets that boost your stats, and rebirth for a permanent multiplier.

### [DegenerateBotWebsite](https://github.com/desidj27/DegenerateBotWebsite)

Dashboard for browsing DegenerateBot’s tracked Discord data. Filter by channel, user, and time range, and look through categories and leaderboards.

### [FafeWebsite](https://github.com/desidj27/FafeWebsite)

A small Next.js countdown page titled Gina Time.

### [cs293_final](https://github.com/desidj27/cs293_final)

An early personal page from a CS course: a short bio, school info, and links to socials.

### [lab7](https://github.com/desidj27/lab7)

A Java Maven lab from a CS course, with JUnit tests.
